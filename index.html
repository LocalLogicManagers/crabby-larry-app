Our story

Meet the claw behind the collection

Crabby Larry started as a nickname before it was a brand — comfortable, coastal fun apparel for people who embrace their pinch of salt life personality.

It grew from a hat, to a tee, to a whole closet's worth: caps, hoodies, tees and totes, each one carrying a little bit of Larry's temperament — a touch feisty, allergic to boring beige, always up for the next trip to the water.

He's molted a few shells getting here, but the mission hasn't changed — coastal life is supposed to be fun, and so is getting dressed for it.
